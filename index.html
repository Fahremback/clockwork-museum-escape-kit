GOOD TO KNOW

Before you start the clock.

Is this a physical escape room kit?

No. It is a digital download with printable PDF files. Nothing is shipped. You print and cut the cards at home.

What do I need to play?

A printer, paper, scissors, and a pencil. No padlocks or specialty props are required. The game host checks each chapter code using the answer key.

How many people can play?

One to six players is ideal. A larger group can use additional printed copies and play in teams.

What age group is this for?

Recommended for ages 10 and up. Younger players can join with help. Each puzzle has two optional hints.

How long does it take?

Allow approximately 30–60 minutes for the Complete edition, depending on your group and hint use. The one-chapter Starter edition is shorter.

Which paper format is included?

Both A4 and US Letter PDFs are included. Print the version that matches your paper at 100% scale.

Can I use it with my class?

Yes, for your own classroom or personal use. Please do not resell or redistribute the original PDF files.
